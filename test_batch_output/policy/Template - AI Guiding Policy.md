**1. Purpose **
The purpose ofthis policy is toestablish a guidingframework forthe ethical, transparent 
and responsible use of artificial intelligence(AI) within [Organization name]. This policy
ensures that AI technologies are deployed in alignment with our organization’s values, legal 
obligations and stakeholder expectations, while mitigating associated risks. 
 
**2. Scope **
This policy is designed to work in accordance with other policies and applies to all AI 
systems and related technologies implemented, developed or maintained by[Organization 
name], including those developed by third-party vendors. Employees and contingent 
workers should follow the requirements in thispolicy to ensureappropriate usage of AI 
systems andtoreduce the likelihood of data confidentiality or integrityrisks. 
 
**3. Definitions **
• **Artificialintelligence (AI): **Technologies thatenable machines to perform tasks that
typically require human intelligence, such as decision-making, language
understanding, visual perception and pattern recognition. 
• **AI model: A** component of an information system that implements AI technology and
uses computational, statistical or machine-learning techniques to produceoutputs
from a given set of inputs. 
• **AI system: **Any data system, software, hardware, application, tool or utility that
operates in whole or in part using AI. 
• **Bias: Any unfair or unequal treatment resulting fromthe design or implementation of**
AI models.

**4. Guidingprinciples **
All AI projects and activities within [Organization name] must align with the following
principles: 
• **Sensitive information:** Never put customer or[Organization name] confidential data
in any unapproved AI tool. 
• **You are responsible and accountablefor the outputs of AI:** It is essential to
thoroughly review and validate the information before relying on it for any decisions
or actions. 
• **You should be able to articulate the risks and potential harms of AI beforeusing**
**it: AI should not be employed in ways that could cause harm (e.g. generating spam) **
or in sensitive areas where therisks are too great (e.g. making personnel-related
decisions). 
 
**5. Basicrules for AI usage **
Generative AI tools, such asMicrosoft Copilot, have been trained to hold human-like
conversations and to understand information based on user input and what is published on 
the internet. 
While AI usage represents an opportunity for internal productivity at[Organization name], 
team membersmustevaluatewhether their intended usage may compromise: 
• [Organization name]'sintellectual property. 
• Confidentiality of[Organization name] or[Organization name]'scustomer data. 
• Privacy of[Organization name]'semployees and customers. 
 
**6. Riskmanagement **
All AI projects must undergo a risk assessment process to identify potential ethical, privacy
and security risks by an [AI Oversight Committee] before project approval. High-risk
projects may require additional oversight, mitigation steps or third-party audits. 
 
The following risk thresholds apply to all AI systems immediately prior to deployment: 
• Systems that remain very high-risk or high-risk post-treatment shall not be allowed to
deploy. 
• Systems deemed moderate-risk post-treatment shall be subject tomore rigorous and
frequent testing and monitoring requirements, including impact assessments, than 
those deemed minimal-risk. 
 
Components of[Organization name]'s risk management processfor AI systems are as
follows: 
1. Impact identification 
2. Risk measurement

**7. Data governance **
All data used in AI systemsshould meet the following criteria: 
• **Data quality andintegrity:** Data used must be accurate, complete and relevant to
the intended purpose. 
• Data privacy: The collection or processing ofpersonal data for AI purposes must
adhere to privacy laws and organizational privacy policies. 
• **Bias andfairnesschecks:** Data must be regularly checked to ensure no
discriminatory patterns exist. 
 
During theearlystagesof a project, teams shall identify, characterize and justify the type
and quantity of data needed for an AI system.  
 
Rigorousexploratory data analysis shall be conducted for each potential data set togain 
insights into underlyingstructures and statistical properties and to assess alignment with 
data fit-for-purpose and quality standards. 
 
General information about every selected and utilized data set shall be logged in an 
enterprise-wide data inventory system, including [e.g. in which projects it is used, internal 
cross-project usage permissions, data source and contact information, data type and
features]. 
 
Systems and standards shall be developed by[e.g. functions such as Information Security] to
manage the secure and organized storage ofdata sets enterprise-wide. 
 
For all data sets from third-partysources, project teams shall follow a standard protocol set
forth by the procurement team to communicate procurement needs during projectscoping
and to allow the procurement team to manage the procurement process. 
 
**8. Secure AI Usage for Internal Corporate Productivity **
Employees and contingent workersmustevaluatewhether intended usage of AI systems
may compromise intellectual property, the confidentiality of[Organization name] or
customer data, or the privacy ofemployees and customers.  
 
Employees and contingent workers are responsible forensuring they:  
• Do not share confidential [Organization name] data or customer data with generative
AI tools.

• Review AI responses for accuracy and reliability and do not trust that thetool will be
accurate or correct. Some AI tools are prone to “hallucinations” (a confident response 
by an AI not justified by facts). This is true of fact-based statements as well as code
generation. 
• In general, avoid sharing personal information of colleagues or customers with AI 
tools unlessan appropriate vendor agreement is in place.  
 
Engaging with AI vendors and systems is subject to standard third-party risk assessment and
due diligence. Depending on risk, certain AI vendors mayrequire additional review to
ensure theymeet requirements for minimizinghallucinations and bias, preserving
intellectual property rights, and maintaining appropriate confidentiality, integrity, and
general security of data. 
 
**9. Secure AI Usage in Product Development **
The purpose ofthis section isto establish guidelines and best practices for the secure
development, deployment, and management of AI systems within the company.  
 
The company’s standards for AI development must include requirements for:  
• Data collection andpreprocessing 
• Model selection andtraining 
• Model evaluation andvalidation 
• Model deployment andmaintenance 
• AI model explainability andtransparency 
• Securecodingpractices 
 
[Organization name] may use AI tools, which include machine learning, generative AI and
Large Language Models (LLM), to enhance the value that our products provide customers.  
The AI models employed by[Organization name] should not be trained on customer data
without the customer’s consent. Furthermore, any content produced by [Organization 
name]'sgenerative AI should be labeled, allowing users to readily identify AI-generated
content.  
 
As with all[Organization name] product functionality, controlsmust be in place toensure
the logical separation of customer data and no unauthorized third parties are provided
access to customer data. The training data and outputs of[Organization name]'sproduct AI 
functions should be tested for reliability and trustworthiness.

**12. Training andawareness **
Employees involved in AI projects shall receive regular trainingon responsible AI use, data
privacy and risk mitigation strategies. The training program will be reviewed annually to
incorporaterelevant updatesto legal and ethical standards relating to AI. 
 
Employeesshall be provided with role- and/or system-specific training and educational 
tools to enable and evaluate their understanding and fulfillment of requirements forAI 
systems. 
 
Employeesshall demonstrate adherence toresponsible use policies and processes, 
including only using approvedAI systems as intended. 
 
[Organization name] shall identify deficienciesin AI capacity and skills, identify which gaps
can be addressed by training and which are relevant to hiring, and seek new talent as
necessary. 
 
**13. Compliance **
The compliance team shall actively monitor andseek tounderstandexisting and emerging
legal and regulatory requirements relevant toall AI activities. 
 
The compliance team shall map the compliance requirementsof each AI system, or
otherwise develop tools and processes, such as checklists or compliance meetings, to
enable technical teams to determine requirements at each stage of a system’s life cycle. 
 
The compliance team shall be ultimately accountable for the perceived or realized
compliance of every system at each stage of the life cycle, from initial projectscoping to
post-deployment or post-sale to potential decommission.
