**1. Purpose** 
This Code of Conduct Policyoutlinesthe expectations foremployee behavior to maintain a
respectful, lawful and productive work environment. All employees, contractors and
representatives of[Organisation Name] must adhere to this policyin accordance with 
relevant E.U. legislation. 
**2. Scope **
This policy applies to all employees, including full-time, part-time, temporary and contract
workers, as well as volunteers and representatives actingon behalf of[Organisation Name]. 
**3. Workplace conduct **
Employeesmust conduct themselves in a professional, ethical and lawful manner at all
times. This includes, but is not limited to: 
• Treating colleagues, clients and stakeholderswith respect and dignity. 
• Refraining from discrimination, harassment and retaliation as prohibited under the
E.U. Charter of Fundamental Rights. 
• Avoiding conflicts of interestthat may compromise the organisation’s integrity. 
• Adhering to workplace safety and health regulations as per the relevant E.U. 
directives.

**4. Anti-discrimination and harassment** 
[Organisation Name] strictly prohibits discrimination and harassment based on sex, race, 
color, ethnic or social origin, genetic features, language, religion or belief, political or any
otheropinion, membership of a national minority, property, birth, disability, age or sexual 
orientation, as outlined in the E.U. Charter of Fundamental Rights. Any violations must be
reported to HR ormanagement immediately. 
**5. Confidentiality anddata protection **
Employeesmust safeguard sensitive and proprietary information, including, but not limited
to, customer data, trade secrets and personal employee information, in compliance with 
relevant privacy laws such as theGeneral Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). 
**6. Workplacesafety andsubstanceuse **
To ensure a safe working environment, employees must: 
• Comply with E.U. health andsafety standards. 
• Report workplace hazards or unsafe conditions immediately. 
• Abstain from substance abuse while on duty. The use of illegal drugs and
unauthorised alcohol consumption on organisation premises is strictly prohibited. 
**7. Use oforganisationproperty **
Employeesmust use organisation resources responsibly. Misuse, theft or unauthorized
access to company property, including electronic systems and intellectual property, is
subject to disciplinary action. 
 
**8. Compliance withlaws andregulations** 
[Organisation Name] must adhereto all applicableE.U. laws, including: 
• The E.U. Charter of Fundamental Rights for fundamental rights protections. 
• The Working Time Directive regarding work hours and rest periods. 
• The GDPR for data protection and privacy. 
• The Health and Safety Framework Directivefor workplace safety. 
It is expectedthatemployees will also adhere to the above compliance obligations, where
applicable.

Employeesmust report any suspected violations of this policyto their supervisor, HR or a

**10. Disciplinaryactions** 
Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in disciplinary action, up to and including
termination of employment. The organization reserves theright to investigate and address
misconduct accordingly. 
**11. Acknowledgment andagreement **
Employeesmust read, understand and comply with this policy. A signed acknowledgment 
form confirming agreement to abide by this Code of Conduct may be required as a
condition of employment.
