1 Introduction 
 
1.1 Purpose 
The purpose of this standard is to set forth the rules for acceptable use of Artificial 
Intelligence (AI) systems based on the Diligent Security Policy. 
 
1.2 Scope 
This Artificial Intelligence Acceptable Use Policy (AIAUP) applies to all employees, contingent 
workers, third-party vendors, and temporary staff with access to Diligent information systems, 
networks, facilities and assets. 
 
2 ArtificialIntelligenceAcceptableUsePolicy (AIAUP) 
To maximize innovation, you should have the flexibility to use AI services as you see fit, so 
long as you comply with the law, customer commitments, internal policy, and ensure your 
usage brings no harm to yourself, the company, or others. 
 
2.1 UserResponsibility 
While AI usage represents an opportunity to increase productivity, you must evaluate if your 
intended usage may compromise: 
 
¥ Diligent’s intellectual property 
¥ the confidentiality of Diligent or Diligent customer data 
¥ the privacy of Diligent employees and customers 
¥ compliance with laws or regulations applicable to sensitive use cases of AI processing 
 
Not all business problems are best suited to an AI-based solution today. The following use 
cases tend to be best suited to AI tooling right now: 
 
¥ workflow automation and coding tasks 
¥ content generation and automation 
¥ text synthesis and summarization 
 
If you are ever in doubt about whether your usage of an AI tool could create a potential 
compliance risk, including for example any risks to Diligent intellectual property or the 
confidentiality of our customers, please open a service request or reach out to 
[SecTicket@diligent.com](mailto:SecTicket@diligent.com) for a quick review.

2.2 Baseline RulesforAcceptable Usage 
§ Do not share confidential Diligent data (C2) or Diligent customer data (C4, C5) with 
generative AI tools unless the tool is procured by Diligent specifically for that purpose. 
§ Review AI responses for accuracy and reliability, do not trust that the tool will be 
accurate or correct. 
§ Any AI tool may “hallucinate” (giving a confident response by an AI that does not seem 
to be justified by facts), but some are more prone than others to “hallucinations”. It is 
prudent to be skeptical of responses where you are not an expert in the field and able 
to validate reliability/accuracy. This is true of fact-based statements as well as code 
generation. 
§ Avoid sharing personal information of colleagues or customers with AI tools. 
§ Ensure the tool you are using is sourced from a supplier that aligns with regulatory and 
reputational expectations and is not developed or hosted in a region that has not been 
approved. Diligent’s status as a U.S. Federal Contractor and major supplier to 
regulated entities may require extra review for certain use cases. 
2.3 Confidentialityand PrivacyofData 
In general, most AI services make no commitments to safeguard the privacy or confidentiality 
of the information shared with them unless this is explicitly covered in an enterprise 
agreement with the AI provider. Services with no contractual commitments to Diligent are 
also unlikely to protect data to the degree we require for most of our day-to-day operations. 
When interacting with AI services, act as if persons outside the company will review your input 
and use appropriate caution when sharing information. 
2.4 Unacceptable UsesofAI 
§ Sharing of regulated data (C3), customer information (C4), or client data (C5) is 
prohibited unless the use case and tool is approved by Legal and Security and 
appropriate vendor agreements are in place with the AI provider. 
§ AI cannot be used for personnel-related decisions at this time. Please reach out to 
Legal and Human Resources with any questions about this. 
§ Applicable laws and regulatory requirements must always be observed when using AI. 
For example, do not: 
o Compromise the privacy of customers, colleagues, or others  
§ Fail to comply with applicable regulations 
§ Promote or engage in any illegal activity, including the exploitation or harm of children 
or the development or distribution of illegal substances, goods, or services

§ Use AI to create deception or manipulation that distorts a person’s behavior in a 
harmful way 
o Exploit any vulnerabilities in systems or services unless authorized to do so 
(testing and validation) 
§ Create or expand biometric databases without thorough review and approval 
o Evaluate or classify individuals based on personal traits (including social scoring 
or predictive profiling) leading to detrimental or unfavorable treatment 
o Infer an individual’s emotions 
§ Categorize individuals based on their biometric data to infer sensitive attributes such as 
their race, political opinions, religious beliefs, or sexual orientation 
§ Causing harm to yourself or others, including using output to: 
o Promote self-harm or violence 
o Injure others or destroy property 
o Scam, defraud, socially engineer, spam, harass, defame, or discriminate against 
others 
§ Collecting, processing, disclosing, inferring or generating personal data without 
complying with applicable legal requirements. 
§ Conducting communications surveillance or unauthorized monitoring of individuals. 
§ Making high-impact automated decisions related to critical infrastructure, safety 
components of products, essential services, or employment. 
§ Generating or promoting disinformation, misinformation, or false online engagement. 
§ Impersonating another individual or organization, or taking unauthorized actions on 
their behalf. 
§ Failing to ensure that AI systems (e.g., chatbots) disclose to people that they are 
interacting with AI. 
§ Misleading or misrepresenting the effectiveness or capability of AI offerings or services 
offered to customers. 
 
Diligent’s Security and Privacy Policies still apply when using AI and must always be observed.

3 Exceptions 
All deviations from this document must be submitted for review by the AI Safety & Ethics 
Committee in the Service Management Portal and in accordance with the Risk Exception 
process 
4 Enforcement 
All employees, contingent workers, third-party vendors, and temporary staff are required to 
comply with this document. Non-compliance may result in disciplinary action up to and 
including termination of employment or contract.
