**1. Purpose **
This policy setsout [Organisation name]’s commitment to conducting business ethically and 
in full compliance with the U.K. Bribery Act 2010. Its purpose is to prevent bribery and
corruption, ensure transparent practices and protect the organization’s reputation and 
stakeholders. 
 
**2. Scope **
This policy applies to: 
• All employees, officers, and directorsof [Organisation name]. 
• Contractors, consultants, intermediaries, joint venture partners and any third parties
representing the organisation. 
• All business activities, whether in the U.K. or abroad. 
 
**3. Policy statement **
[Organisation name] prohibits all forms of bribery and corruption. No person acting for or
on behalf of theorganisation may, directly or indirectly: 
• Offer, give, request or accept a bribe of any kind. 
• Make facilitation payments (small payments intended to speed up routine
government actions), which are strictly prohibited under the U.K. Bribery Act. 
• Provide or accept hospitality, giftsor expenses intended to improperly influence a
decision. 
• Fail to prevent bribery in any part of the organisation or its associated persons.

**4. Definitions **
• **Bribery**: Offering, giving, requestingor accepting an advantage to influence an 
action improperly. 
• **Advantage**: Includes money, gifts, hospitality, services, employment opportunities or
anything else ofvalue. 
• **Associated persons**: Anyone who performsservices for oron behalf of the
organisation, e.g., employees, contractors, agents, subsidiaries. 
 
**5. Gifts, hospitality and expenses **
Reasonable and proportionate hospitality orgifts are permitted only if they are: 
• Intended to build legitimate business relationships, not to secure an improper
advantage. 
• Of modestvalue, infrequent and transparent. 
• Approved in advance when required and recorded accurately in company records. 
 
**6. Charitable and political contributions **
Charitable donations must be genuine and not used as a form of bribery. Political donations
made on behalf of theorganisation are prohibited unless specifically approved by the
board and compliant with applicable laws. 
 
**7. Facilitation payments **
Facilitation payments are prohibited under the U.K. Bribery Act. Employeesmust refuse
such payments and report any requests immediately. 
 
**8. Third-party relationships **
The organisation may be held liable for bribery committed by third parties actingon its
behalf. Therefore: 
• Due diligencemust be conducted beforeengaging third parties. 
• Contracts must include anti-bribery clauses. 
• Paymentsmust be transparent, proportionateand properly documented. 
 
**9. Recordkeeping **
All transactions must be fully and accurately recorded. False or misleading entries are
prohibited. Financial controls must ensure transparency and detect improper payments. 
 
**10. Training and awareness **
All employees and associated persons will receivetraining on this policy and the U.K. 
Bribery Act to ensure awareness ofobligations and risks.
