**1. Purpose** 
[Organization Name] is committed to conducting business with integrity and transparency. 
This Conflict of Interest Policyoutlinesguidelines to identify, disclose and manage conflicts
of interest thatmay arise in the course of business operations. 
**2. Scope **
This policy applies to all employees, officers, directors, contractors and any individuals
acting on behalf of[Organization Name]. It covers all situations where personal, financial or
other interestsmay conflict with the duties owed to theorganization. 
**3. Definition of conflict of interest **
A conflict of interest arises when an individual's personal interests interfere, or appear to
interfere, with the interestsof[Organization Name]. Examples include, but are not limited to: 
• Personal financial interests in vendors, suppliers or competitors. 
• Engaging in business activities that compete with thoseof [Organization Name]. 
• Hiring or supervising close relatives orfriends. 
• Receivinggifts, favors, or benefits from individuals or entities conducting business
with [Organization Name]. 
• Using confidential information for personal gain.

**4. Disclosure and reporting **
• Employees and affiliates must disclose any actual or potential conflictsof interest to
their supervisor, HR or a designated compliance officer. 
• Disclosure must be made in writing and as soon as the potential conflict is identified. 
• Periodic conflict of interest declarations may be required toensure ongoing
compliance. 
**5. Management and resolution **
• The organization will review disclosed conflicts and determine appropriate measures
to mitigate risks. 
• Employeesmay be required to recuse themselves from decision-making processes
related to the conflict. 
• In certain cases, divestment from conflicting interestsmay be necessary. 
**6. Consequences of non-compliance **
• Failure to disclose or appropriately manage a conflict of interest mayresult in 
disciplinary action, including termination of employment. 
• Legal consequencesmay apply in cases of fraud, bribery or unethical behavior. 
**7. Review andupdates **
• This policy will be reviewed periodically toensure alignment with legal and ethical 
standards. 
• Employees are required to acknowledge and adhere to this policy as part of their
employment obligations. 
**8. Acknowledgment **
By engaging with [Organization Name], individuals acknowledge their responsibility to
identify, disclose and manage conflicts of interest in accordance with this policy.
